is a PhD student in the CEE Department at CSU. Bill's dissertation research will focus on climate change impacts on transboundary Rivers and international water-sharing agreements. Bill recently retired from his position with the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, where over his career he served as the Senior Water Resources Engineer at the U.S. Embassy Brasilia Brazil, U.S. Embassy Islamabad Pakistan, and U.S. Embassy Kabul Afghanistan. Prior to his work abroad, Bill has extensive experience working on hydrologic and hydraulic analysis of large river systems, including serving as a Senior Water Resources Manager at the USACE working on reservoir forecasting and operations in the Missouri River Basin. Bill has a B.S. degree in Civil Engineering from the University of Nebraska and an M.S. degree in Hydrology from Colorado State University. You can learn more about Bill at his [website](https://www.williamdoan.org/).
